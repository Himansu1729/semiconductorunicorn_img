## Caption

About 44% of a grown silicon oxide layer sinks into the wafer. 🔬

When silicon is heated in oxygen or steam, SiO2 forms by using up the wafer's own silicon. Grow 100 nm of oxide and roughly 44 nm of silicon is consumed (sources round it to 44-46%), while the rest rises above the original surface. That moves the Si/SiO2 interface down into the crystal, something every process engineer has to budget for.
