## Caption

A Huawei HBM design with 4,096 data pins is on the ISSCC 2027 program. 🧠

The draft program for ISSCC (Feb 14-18, 2027, San Francisco) puts a memory session on Feb 16. Key numbers:
• Huawei: custom HBM, 64 channels, 4,096 data pins, 5-layer wafer-on-wafer hybrid bonding (JEDEC HBM4 uses a 2,048-bit interface)
• Samsung: 48GB HBM4E at 17Gb/s per pin
• CXMT: 32Gb vertical-gate DDR5 at 9.2Gb/s per pin
• SK hynix: 16Gb vertical-gate DDR5
• Overall: China has 124 of 274 accepted papers (45.3%), not counting invited ones

These are research papers, not product launches, and the draft program can still change.

VLSI term: hybrid bonding joins wafers or dies by bonding copper pads and insulator surfaces directly, with no solder bumps.

## Alt text

1. Cover graphic: Huawei takes the HBM stage. The ISSCC 2027 program lists a Huawei custom HBM paper right after Samsung's HBM4E, plus new DRAM work from CXMT and SK hynix, November 25, 2026, with a chip illustration.
2. Graphic: ISSCC, a leading IEEE chip-design conference, meets February 14-18, 2027 in San Francisco; its draft program has a session on SRAM, HBM and DRAM on February 16. These are research papers, not product launches.
3. Graphic: 4,096. Huawei's paper title lists a custom HBM with 64 channels, 4,096 data pins, memory controllers and 5-layer wafer-on-wafer hybrid bonding, versus 32 channels and a 2,048-bit interface in the JEDEC HBM4 standard. No capacity, speed or bandwidth figure is listed.
4. Graphic: 17 gigabits per second. Samsung lists a 48GB HBM4E at 17Gb/s per pin, compared with 11.7Gb/s, up to 13Gb/s, announced for its HBM4 in February 2026. Bandwidth math: 2,048 pins times 17Gb/s divided by 8 is about 4.35 TB/s per stack.
5. Graphic: DRAM going vertical. CXMT lists a 32Gb DDR5 at 9.2Gb/s per pin with vertical-surrounding-gate transistors and copper hybrid bonding; SK hynix lists a 16Gb DDR5 with vertical-gate cells, control circuits stacked over them and bonded wafers.
6. Graphic: 124. Korean reports count 274 accepted papers, not counting invited ones, from 1,029 submissions: China 124 (45.3%), US 49, South Korea 45. In memory, South Korea led with 12 of 28 and China had 8.
7. Graphic: VLSI term, hybrid bonding. It joins wafers or dies face to face by bonding flat insulating surfaces and copper pads directly, with no solder bumps. HBM is a stack of DRAM dies with a very wide data path.
